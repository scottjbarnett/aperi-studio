Digital Rights

Data sovereignty, consent, identity, and agentic AI.

What would digital rights look like if they were enforceable by tools, agents, and institutions - not just written in policies?

Concept room

The public-facing frame

Aperi Studio explores whether agentic AI could help people monitor, manage, negotiate, and enforce their data rights over time.

This work develops ideas that have previously sat under the internal working name CivicOS. For public-facing work, the clearer frame is digital rights infrastructure.

The problem

Data sovereignty is often discussed as a principle. The harder question is how it becomes operational.

If individuals are expected to manage consent, identity, privacy, permissions, data access, and institutional relationships across many systems, they may need trusted agents that can act on their behalf.
