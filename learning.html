Aperi Learning

Domain-specific AI and education systems.

Bounded AI learning tools, curriculum-aware agents, student data sovereignty, and education infrastructure.

Concept development

The talking textbook

Aperi Learning explores bounded AI systems for education: tools that behave less like open-ended chatbots and more like interactive study guides within a defined learning domain.

Curriculum as a boundary layer

Educational AI may become safer and more useful when constrained by domain, syllabus, standard, age level, and learning objective.

Student data sovereignty

Learning systems should not require students to give up long-term control of their educational data in exchange for assistance.
